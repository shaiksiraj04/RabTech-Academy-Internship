## Research Questions

The analysis will investigate:

1. Whether `total_bill` follows a normal distribution.
2. Whether `total_bill` differs between smokers and non-smokers.
3. Whether average `total_bill` differs across days.
4. Whether gender and meal time affect `tip`.
