# Research Question

## Population

College students participating in the study.

## Exposure

Participation and frequency of structured programming practice.

## Comparator

Students with lower or limited programming-practice participation.

## Outcome

Programming assessment performance.

## Observation Window

12 weeks.

## Research Question

Among college students, is higher participation in structured programming practice associated with better programming assessment performance over a 12-week observation period?

## Null Hypothesis (H0)

There is no statistically significant association between structured programming-practice participation and programming assessment performance over the 12-week observation period.

## Alternative Hypothesis (H1)

Higher participation in structured programming practice is associated with better programming assessment performance over the 12-week observation period.

## Research Design

The analysis will examine the predefined relationship between programming-practice participation and programming assessment performance.

The research question and primary analysis plan will be defined before inspecting outcome patterns in the real dataset.
